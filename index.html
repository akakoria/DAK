Welcome to Kakoria-NanoTech Lab

We develop structured polymeric and nanostructured materials for applications in environmental stewardship, energy safety, and sustainable resource recovery. Our core research vectors unite scalable materials manufacturing with computational modeling and state-of-the-art characterization.

🌱

Environmental Remediation & Water Stewardship

  • • Acid mist mitigation in copper electrowinning
  • • Heavy metal & acid mine drainage remediation
  • • Tertiary wastewater treatment & reuse
  • • Air & water purification (PFAS, VOCs, PM)
⚡

Energy Storage Safety

  • • EV battery thermal runaway diagnostics
  • • Thermal, mechanical & chemical fire studies
  • • Off-gas analysis (FTIR) & gas detection
  • • Flame retardant & fire resistant materials
♻️

Resource Recovery & Circular Economy

  • • Hydrometallurgy & copper recovery
  • • Spent Lithium-ion battery material recycling
  • • Repurposing plastic waste into nanofibers
  • • Multiphase transport & process optimization

About PI

Dr. Ashish Kakoria leads research at the intersection of functional fiber manufacturing, polymer science, energy safety, and environmental technology. His research portfolio emphasizes scalable nanomanufacturing strategies including supersonic nanoblowing, solution blowing, and electrospinning.
